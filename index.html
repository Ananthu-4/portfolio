About

I'm an aspiring APM, not an experienced product manager. I'm building practical product skills by taking real problems and working through them the way a product team would: who is affected, what we know versus assume, what to build first, and how we'd know it worked.

What draws me to product work:

  • Understanding user problems and unmet needs
  • Brainstorming and evaluating product features
  • Turning ideas into structured requirements
  • Designing simple, intuitive experiences
  • Collaborating across design, engineering, and business
  • Using data and feedback to improve products

I hold a BSc in Computer Science and am completing an MCA (2024 to 2026), with hands-on work in Python, Java, React and Django. That foundation helps me ask better questions about feasibility and trade-offs when working with engineers.
